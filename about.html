THE APPROACH

Understand the operation
before suggesting solutions.

No two dairy farms have exactly the same needs. Herd size, animal health, feed availability, infrastructure, labour, milk collection and local market conditions all shape what is practical.

Asha Dairy Consulting is being developed as a personal, farm-focused consulting service for farmers and dairy businesses in Pune and Maharashtra. The aim is to help people think through their challenges, compare options and identify sensible next steps.

Our principle

Advice should be clear, realistic and relevant to the farm—not based on promises or generic formulas.
